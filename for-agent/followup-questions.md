## Part 2. Specific questions

7. Do mCherry+ cells give rise to other cell types after injury? If so, which types, and at which time point does each become a distinct population? How do you tell lineage-derived cells from contaminating cells of the same type?
8. Is there an intermediate state, or a point where the lineage splits? If so, when, and what defines it?
9. When does proliferation start, peak and end in the labeled lineage? Which genes support your answer?
10. Which transcription factors change in biliary-lineage cells over the time course? Which go up and which go down? Do any change before other cell identity changes you found?
11. Which gene programs (for example GO terms) dominate biliary-lineage cells at each time point? If you found new cell types in the lineage, which programs dominate the earliest ones? Say what comparison you used for each (for example each time point against the one before, or against mock).
12. Which growth factor ligands change earliest after ablation, and in which cell types? Which receptor pathways could they act through?
13. Do biliary marker genes change after ablation? In which cells, and when?
14. Do all biliary cells respond to injury, or only a subset? What is your evidence?
15. Are the uninjured hepatocytes one population or more than one? What distinguishes them? Can you say where in the liver each sits?
16. Are there immune cells in the time-course samples? Do their numbers or states change after ablation? How much can you trust that?
17. Which intracellular signaling pathways are likely active in biliary-lineage cells after ablation? What in the data suggests it, and what can transcript data not tell you?
18. If you found hepatocyte-like cells in the labeled lineage, how do the earliest ones compare with those at the last time point, and with uninjured hepatocytes? What might explain the differences?
